History

A well-chosen typeface defines a design’s tone. Nunito Sans achieves this by balancing readability and structure, ensuring effective communication. This typeface is a neo-grotesque sans-serif created by Jacques Le Bailly in 2017. It serves as a counterfont to Vernon Adams’s original Nunito, replacing its rounded features with a sans-serif structure. Although classified as a neo-grotesque typeface, its open letterforms and slightly more humanist qualities give it a warmer, more readable character compared to stricter neo-grotesques. This redesign allowed designers to use the same family font with a different tone. In February 2023, expanding into a variable typeface featuring four axes: weight, width, x-height, and optical size. As it is part of a broader industry shift toward variable fonts. Nunito Sans is available for download on Google Fonts and is suitable for personal and professional projects.

Supported Languages include:

Latin, Cyrillic and Greek. Africa (244), Americas (117), Asia (100), Europe (154) and Oceanica (28).
